# NexDoc Slide Deck Anthology

Create exactly 15 widescreen 16:9 slides. Each slide is a standalone representative page from a different fictional presentation—not one continuous presentation.

## Global rules

- Start immediately with slide 1. No anthology cover, contents, intro, outro, or shared template.
- One supplied specimen per slide, in the supplied order. Never continue a specimen.
- Every slide must look like it came from a different senior presentation design team: unique palette, typography, grid, density, visual language, and storytelling device.
- Use a mix of hero photography, product renders, diagrams, maps, timelines, charts, editorial typography, tables, and data visualization.
- Favor realistic interior slides over generic title pages. Each slide should communicate one clear idea in under five seconds while retaining rewarding close-up detail.
- Enforce the supplied hex palettes. Adjacent slides must be unmistakably different at thumbnail size.
- Avoid repeated card grids, excessive rounded rectangles, fake browser chrome, generic stock-business imagery, tiny text, and decorative gradients.
- Give the final output in PDF
- All brands, organizations, people, figures, and claims are fictional demonstration content.
- Do not place NexDoc branding or anthology labels inside the slides.
